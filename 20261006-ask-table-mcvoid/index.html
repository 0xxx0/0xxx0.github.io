the words that hide the ask

Work-speak isn't lying to you — it's just compressed. Decode the compression, not the person.

pls adviseThey want your recommendation, not a summary. Give one option and say why.
pls confirmThey want a yes/no and a date. Nothing else.
for visibilityNo action. Read it and move on. Safe to ignore.
looping inSomeone new is now responsible. Not you.
flaggingThere's a risk and they want it on record. Reply so it's on yours too.
action:The only line in the message that's actually a request. Everything else is context.
prev discussedThey believe you already agreed to this. Before you reply, go find that conversation.
as discussedSame, but weaker. Verify before acting.
trsT / t&b / asapChat shorthand. trsT = “trust” as in “assume”. t&b = to be confirmed. When you can't decode it — that's the signal to ask.
EOD / EOW / COBEnd of day / end of week / close of business. Always ask whose timezone — in APAC it is often not yours.
P0 / P1 / P2Severity. P0 = drop everything. P1 = today. P2 = this week.
blockerSomeone is stuck and waiting on you. These outrank your own plan.
bandwidthDo I have time. “No bandwidth” is a complete sentence.
socialiseTell people before deciding. Not a decision yet.
park / shelfStop, revisit later. Ask when — or it never comes back.
RFIRequest for Information. A formal ask, usually with a deadline and a record. Treat it as a document, not a chat.

the traps — the four that actually burn people

trap 1 Legal hold does not cover Slack Connect → Outside counsel in a shared channel? That chat can vanish under a hold. Ask IT, in writing: “Does our legal hold cover Slack Connect and bot-posted messages?” You want a yes/no you can quote.
trap 2 Privilege is lost by forwarding → The moment you forward counsel's advice into a wider channel, you can waive it. Keep advice in the smallest channel that can act on it.
trap 3 The regulator gets the whole thread, not your summary → In a production, what's discoverable is the conversation — not the tidy version you wrote later. Write every message as if it will be read out.
trap 4 “Delete after reading” is a record → Disappearing messages + a retention obligation = the worst of both. If a matter is live, turn disappearing off in that channel.
