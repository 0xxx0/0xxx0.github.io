## Objective and boundary

Reduce the amount the operator must read/manipulate while keeping exact objects,
source descent, history and lawful native actions reachable. Assumptions: current
native owners remain; source archives stay intact; geometry does not create
relationships; physical outcomes require physical observations.

**Diagnosis (inference):** capable mechanisms survived, but each projection grew
its own controls and promises. The resulting search/briefing cost consumes the
benefit. A common bar is useful only if it actually reaches the complete source
set and preserves the addressed object. “Nearly lossless” here means recoverable
source identity and hidden detail, not simultaneous display of every distinction.
No numerical information-loss or cognitive-load bound is claimed.

## Implemented minimal organism

1. **Rotary bar:** ordinal rows wrap around a horizontal aperture. At most three
   are visible; all eight views and all matched addresses remain in the sequence.
   Exact active ordinal/total and full label/address accompany the geometry.
   Arrow keys, Home/End, wheel and swipe rotate; rotation never selects or edits.
   Tap/Enter selects. UNFOLD restores the native list, import/export and contracts.
   Slash-view commands work through the same native buttons. FIND's old 12-item
   truncation and the compositor's eight-item truncation are removed.
2. **FOVEA context:** explicit focus, its declared ancestor/direct-child context,
   and unrelated periphery receive distinct contrast. Nothing is removed,
   disabled or reordered. Keyboard focus restores full contrast. Desktop reduced
   motion SNAP and touch PIN share the same rule; OFF removes the projection.
3. **Semantic texture:** FOVEA reuses FIELD's already-derived route-local signal,
   displays its literal decoder and retained-source count. MIXED means source-owned
   co-presence, not inferred disagreement, urgency or mental state. Texture in the
   readout is static. No extra animated/decorative moiré layer was added.
4. **Bounded repair:** Ω question IDs are escaped before HTML rendering; the
   existing lens-proof files are restored to the route's evidence pointers.
   A measured mutation-stamp defect is repaired: mixed Git timezones are
   normalized before chronology comparison and emission. An isolated real-Git
   fixture proves the later instant wins and a stale stamp still rejects.

Source files: `interphase/compositor*`, `interphase/app.mjs`, `fovea-context.js`,
`fovea-lens.js`, root CSS in `index.html`. Native effects, history/source import
