## Working question

Does the useful mechanism survive when the religious/poetic language is removed?

Candidate transformation:

```
CLAIM / POSSESS / DEFEND
        ↓
DISTINGUISH WHAT IS ACTUALLY OURS TO ACT ON
        ↓
RELEASE UNOWNABLE OUTCOME / IDENTITY / OTHER
        ↓
KEEP RESPONSIBILITY / CARE / SKILL
        ↓
ACT
        ↓
OBSERVE CONSEQUENCE
        ↓
RETURN
```

Plain compression:

> Stop treating role, outcome, meaning, or another person as property. Keep the part that remains your responsibility and act on that.

The source phrase remains a situated mnemonic. It is not required vocabulary.

## Plain-language ablation

This is a **desk ablation**, not a user study. It compares whether the transformation remains useful without the source vocabulary.

### Conditions

**A — charged/source language**
- “die before you die”
- “let the false owner die”
- “fanāʾ → baqāʾ”
- “lose the throne; keep the responsibility”

**B — plain language**
- “separate what you can act on from what you cannot own”
- “stop defending the label; describe the current state”
- “release the result; keep the obligation”
- “do the smallest responsible next action and check what changed”

### Cases

| Ordinary case | Charged/source reading | Plain reading | Ablation result |
|---|---|---|---|
| loose household fault | puncture grand narrative; return to material reality | check the small physical cause before inventing a large explanation | **PLAIN PASSES**; source language adds memorability only |
| delayed reply from another person | release ownership of the other person’s response | state what you need, send/draft only under authority, do not infer meaning from silence | **PLAIN PASSES BETTER**; avoids spiritualizing another person |
| stuck project | relinquish attachment to ideal outcome / identity | identify the next controllable move, do it, inspect consequence | **PLAIN PASSES** |
| identity label (“I am X”) | let the claimant / false owner loosen | rewrite essence claim as current observation or role; test against behavior | **PLAIN PASSES** |
| handoff / delegation | stewardship rather than ownership | preserve source, authority boundary, state, unresolved questions, and return path | **PLAIN PASSES**; operational structure already exists elsewhere |

### Result

The mechanism survives the ablation.

The charged language adds:
- compression,
- mnemonic force,
- emotional/aesthetic salience,
- lineage-specific meaning.

It does **not** add a required operational step.

Therefore:

1. Do **not** promote “die before you die”, fanāʾ/baqāʾ, or “de-appropriation” as universal system vocabulary.
2. Keep source traditions and poetic formulations available as unequal projections / situated mnemonics.
3. Transfer only the plain mechanism when it earns a named use.
4. A future empirical comparison may still show that a particular framing improves recall, courage, or adherence; that would be evidence about the projection, not proof of a universal ontology.

## Failure checks

Reject or demote this transfer when it:
- makes an ordinary task less clear than plain language;
- turns another person into an object of one’s “practice”;
- disguises avoidance/passivity as nonattachment;
- reduces responsibility rather than false ownership;
- creates a new term without a new discrimination;
- becomes an aesthetic explanation with no observable consequence;
- forces a project to adopt spiritual vocabulary to access an already-existing operation.

## Promotion gate

Current verdict: **CANDIDATE MECHANISM; NO CORE PROMOTION.**

Promote only if at least one recipient shows a missing function that this mechanism resolves better than its existing vocabulary, and leaves a recipient-side RETURN.
