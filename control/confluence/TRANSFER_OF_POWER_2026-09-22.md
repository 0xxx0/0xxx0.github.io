## III. THE LESSONS — WHAT THE WORK LEARNED ABOUT ITSELF

The Sovereign Node convergence (2026-08-26) named the failure precisely:

> **The recurring failure is status laundering, not forgetting.** A discussion becomes a proposal, a proposal becomes a generated installer, and the installer later gets remembered as a deployed system.

Three further lessons:

1. **Performance during practice is not learning.** Learning is retained change measured later. Applies to agents as much as humans.
2. **The ratchet must remain after the session.** A log, fixture, card, benchmark, or tested procedure survives every excursion. Without it, the session evaporates.
3. **Near transfer is normal; far transfer is exceptional.** Design bridge tasks. Do not assume general enhancement.

And the governing metric:

> **RETURN LATENCY** — can a fresh operator recover what is true, uncertain, promised, currently material, and next-testable within ten minutes?

This is more diagnostic than board completeness or memory volume.
