## The law

> **NINE-GATE LAW (canon).** Before a run's movement may enter canon it passes the Nine-Gate circuit of ONE RETURN: eight enacted validity gates — PROVENANCE (the source names its origin), TRUTH (no move is invented; the field is let move), COMPRESSION (the object survives another reading with declared loss), RETRIEVAL (the address is recovered after leaving it), OPERATION (a real state change is made), MEASURE (quantity is held to a declared band and becomes evidence), TRANSFER (the operator works in a second context), RESILIENCE (identity survives a failed coordinate) — followed by the ninth gate, RETURN: retrieval of the starting coordinate after all eight proofs, emitting an inspectable Return Artifact. Each proof records Gate, cell token, method, step, elapsed time and causal note; touching a gate without satisfying its law does nothing; gates are added only with a new protocol handler and proof fixture, never as names alone. The same non-collapse law runs at other depths: house epistemic classes (OBSERVED / INFERRED / STALE / UNAVAILABLE) grade state claims, and the shopping lifecycle (NEED → FOUND → VERIFY → WATCH → BOUGHT → RECEIVED → TESTED → ADOPTED) gates material promotion; no evidence state promotes without a receipt, and no status is laundered upward by repetition.
