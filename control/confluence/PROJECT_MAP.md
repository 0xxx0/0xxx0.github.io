## Management rule

For every material change ask only:

1. **Which family changed?**
2. **What became possible that was impossible before?**
3. **What evidence exists now?**
4. **What can be parked or deleted from attention?**
5. **What is the one next conversion?**

If a proposed change cannot answer one of those, it is probably coordination churn rather than progress.
