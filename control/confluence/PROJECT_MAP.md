## What the system now defends for us

The repo increasingly enforces these instead of relying on memory:

- **source ≠ projection**
- **selection ≠ effect**
- **calculation/support ≠ permission**
- **route/visibility/recent commit ≠ priority**
- **candidate PR ≠ current truth**
- **recovery ≠ active work**
- **receipt ≠ lived value**
- **RETURN closes one move; it does not authorize the sequel**

That is the management advance: we do not need a larger planner. We need a smaller selector over a system that preserves its own boundaries.
