## Reconstructed invariant — persistence under transformation

This is reconstruction, not an exactly recovered historical law.

For any transformation ask:

- what may change?
- what must survive?
- what may be discarded?
- what must remain addressable?
- what interface/invariant crosses the change?
- what proves survival?
- where does RETURN land?

Examples:

- Game of Names: label may change; referent/provenance survive.
- Projection Harness: coordinates/view may change; address/relations/ports survive.
- WRAP PROOF: embedding may change; declared topology/identity relation survives.

- CONTINUITY: summary/interpretation may change; raw source/provenance/unresolved distinctions survive.
- LACONIC: wording/compression may change; intended move/provenance/permission boundary survive.
- INTERPHASE: projection may change; host truth/address/authority survive.

- BODY/FIT: module/loadout/sensor state may change; body address/source identity/RETURN survive.
