## Language / corpus anchors

### Wenyan / 文言

Public surface: https://wy-lang.org/

Role here: **classical-language ↔ executable-code projection**.

Do not treat Wenyan as a poetry form. Our historical runtime work had parser/version drift; verified syntax must remain atomic and version-aware.

### COPE

Public source: https://github.com/LingDong-/cope

Role here: **regulated classical-Chinese verse editor donor**.

High-value mechanisms: tone-pattern/error checks, rhyme dictionaries, line-wise corpus neighbours, edit suggestions, editor-first orientation.

### Chinese Text Project / CText

Public corpus: https://ctext.org/
API: https://ctext.org/tools/api

Role here: **source/corpus/evidence substrate**.

CTP URNs, full text and text-analysis tooling can support provenance, lookup and corpus neighbourhoods. Corpus recurrence or proximity is not automatic semantic/literary authority.

### English Jueju

Reference: University of Oklahoma / Newman Prize for English Jueju.

Role here: **bounded transfer adapter now implemented in Verse Copilot 0.3.1 candidate**.

The current adapter declares 4×5 or 4×7 monosyllabic English, visible 2+3 / 2+2+3 grouping, an approximate AABA rhyme check, and 起→承→轉→合 as author judgment. It is sourced to University of Oklahoma / Newman Prize teaching materials and explicitly does **not** claim historical Tang phonology/prosody. Deeper sound/meaning parallelism remains OPEN rather than silently approximated.
