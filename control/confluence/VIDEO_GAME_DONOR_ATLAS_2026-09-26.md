# VIDEO GAME DONOR ATLAS — 2026-09-26

**Status:** RECOVER / QUARRY / TRANSFER
**Role:** mechanism quarry for CONFLUENCE / FIELD.
**Non-goal:** do not copy copyrighted assets, dialogue, proprietary code, level content, or branded visual identity. Steal mechanisms, constraints, interaction grammars, failure-handling, information architecture, and proof structures.

## Selection rule

A game earns transfer only when it offers a mechanism that solves a current FIELD problem, survives extraction from fiction/art style, preserves source/domain authority, can be stated as a testable invariant, and does not require a new sovereign app.

## 1. CITIZEN SLEEPER — bounded capacity + clocks + drives

Mechanism: each cycle exposes a limited hand of dice; the player allocates known-quality capacity to actions. Story progress is represented through clocks/drives rather than one quest tree. The design turns precarity into a visible action budget.

Steal:
- CAPACITY TOKENS rather than an infinite task list.
- CLOCK = accumulating consequence, not merely deadline.
- DRIVE = persistent direction, distinct from immediate action.
- Known uncertainty before commitment: see die value, then choose where to spend it.
- Characters/threads stay separate but coexist on one station map.

FIELD targets: Dayline bounded capacity markers; CARE/training effort/dose distinct from urgency; CURRENT direction distinct from next move; human/agent orchestration assigns scarce high-confidence execution deliberately.

Do not steal: do not gamify life with survival meters; do not turn dice quality into health/productivity score.

Sources: https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/citizen-sleeper/ ; https://www.originstory.show/episodes/citizen-sleeper ; https://www.fellowtravellerpresskit.com/citizen-sleeper

## 2. OUTER WILDS — knowledge is progression / rumor graph

Mechanism: world resets while knowledge persists. Ship log offers place/map organization and rumor/relation organization.

Steal:
- WORLD RESET != KNOWLEDGE RESET.
- One object can have PLACE VIEW and RELATION VIEW.
- A graph earns display when it answers what is connected/unresolved.
- Re-entry should reopen at known unresolved edges.

FIELD targets: Continuity/recovery; FIELD INDEX map vs unresolved-relation projection; ONE RETURN preserves learned constraints after failed branches.

Do not steal: no universal conspiracy graph; line-on-screen is not relation proof.

Source: Alex Beachum discussion of ship-log map/rumor modes: https://www.listennotes.com/podcasts/the-fourth-curtain/outer-wilds-alex-beachums-SI6GdyaUxkm/

## 3. RETURN OF THE OBRA DINN — identity as the hard problem

Mechanism: the deeper task is assigning stable identities to many visually present but unnamed people; the logbook combines identity and fate across scenes.

Steal:
- Identity may remain unresolved while observations accumulate.
- FACE / INSTANCE != NAME / IDENTITY.
- A boring matrix can beat an elaborate ontology when disambiguation is the task.
- Confirmation should require mutually supporting evidence, not lucky resemblance.

FIELD targets: Game of Names, CONTINUITY aliases, BODYFIELD donor-instance vs concept vs self, artifact census.

Do not steal: no alias auto-resolution from resemblance.

Sources: https://arstechnica.com/video/watch/obra-dinn-war-stories/ ; https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/for-lucas-pope-i-return-of-the-obra-dinn-i-was-a-bunch-of-appealing-design-problems

## 4. THE CASE OF THE GOLDEN IDOL — observation → assertion

Mechanism: exploration collects a constrained vocabulary; the player constructs explicit assertions separately.

Steal:
- OBSERVE and ASSERT are different modes/authorities.
- Vocabulary bank constrains syntax without choosing the answer.
- Partial validation can reveal proximity without revealing the wrong slot.
- Intermediate deductions avoid one giant all-or-nothing proof.

FIELD targets: CONTINUITY candidate facts → reviewed claim; FIELD INTAKE observed token bank → structured assertion; CARE observation vs interpretation; PROOF subclaims before global PASS.

Source: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/case-of-the-golden-idol

## 5. HEAVEN'S VAULT — provisional translation + repeated confirmation

Mechanism: candidate translations remain uncertain; repeated contextual evidence can confirm them; knowledge can persist across replay.

Steal:
- GLOSS? before CANONICAL MEANING.
- Repetition across unequal contexts raises confidence.
- Unknown is playable; incomplete understanding need not block action.
- Component resemblance may propose families without proving identity.

FIELD targets: Game of Names, Glyph Atlas, multilingual seals, recovery confidence, proposal → corroboration → promotion.

Do not steal: confidence is not certainty; resemblance is not lineage.

Source: https://ibibaxterwebb.wordpress.com/2024/09/11/e2-heavens-vault-conlangs-and-translation-as-a-game-mechanic/

## 6. CHANTS OF SENNAAR — editable hypotheses + world verification

Mechanism: provisional glyph meanings are recorded, recur in multiple contexts, then a notebook page can validate them. Puzzles both require language and teach language.

Steal:
- HYPOTHESIS → USE IN WORLD → SECOND CONTEXT → VALIDATE.
- Notebook retains editable hypotheses, not only solved facts.
- Unknown terms should recur in more than one context when possible.
- Learning tool and action tool can be the same object.

FIELD targets: Game of Names; seal/glyph/multilingual composition; research hypothesis ledger; HOUSE/FIELD symbols earn meaning through use.

Do not steal: not every ambiguity must resolve; raw occurrences must never be silently rewritten.

Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/immersing-players-in-the-culture-of-a-people-with-language-puzzler-chants-of-sennaar ; https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/deep-dive-the-visual-tapestry-of-i-chants-of-sennaar-i-

## 7. TUNIC — latent capability unlocked by knowledge

Mechanism: manual pages reveal verbs/capabilities that often existed all along. Progress comes from learning the system rather than acquiring a new power.

Steal:
- CAPABILITY EXISTS can differ from CAPABILITY KNOWN.
- Documentation can be an interactive artifact rather than help text.
- Reveal just enough for experimentation; later knowledge can reinterpret earlier places.
- A recovered fragment can expand reachable operations without changing underlying code/state.

FIELD targets: FIELD INDEX as recoverable manual; capability index AVAILABLE / DISCOVERED / PROVED; Sleeper operators; House/physical operation grammar beside objects.

Do not steal: never hide essential safety constraints as secrets.

Sources: https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/designing-content-for-no-one-an-interview-with-the-team-behind-tunic ; https://www.redbull.com/my-en/tunic-video-game-developer-interview

## 8. HADES — failure advances continuity

Mechanism: death is not game-over; runs can advance relationships, dialogue, knowledge and permanent state; narrative reacts to prior events.

Steal:
- FAILED ATTEMPT → RETURN WITH DIFFERENCE.
- Failure should generate residue/receipt useful to next attempt.
- Re-entry should respond to specific prior events rather than replay generic onboarding.
- Return through a rich home/base rather than blank reset.

FIELD targets: ONE RETURN; agent/code runs; training/self-experiment; project continuity.

Do not steal: no XP-for-failure theatre when nothing was learned.

Sources: https://blog.playstation.com/2021/08/05/the-origins-of-hades-out-next-week-on-ps5-ps4 ; https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/how-supergiant-weaves-narrative-rewards-into-i-hades-i-cycle-of-perpetual-death

## 9. DEATHLOOP — temporal addressing + knowledge survives reset

Mechanism: areas occupy time periods; leaving advances time; learned codes/schedules let later loops construct better routes.

Steal:
- Address = PLACE × TIME.
- A route may be impossible until a dependency is learned elsewhere/elsewhen.
- Durable progression can be knowledge, not inventory.
- Replay becomes planning when schedule is stable enough to exploit.

FIELD targets: Dayline/Atlas; House routines; recovery asks when was this true; story scenes across repeated passes.

Sources: https://www.gameinformer.com/preview/2020/07/10/answers-to-our-biggest-questions ; https://www.gameinformer.com/exclusive-interview/2021/03/25/what-you-need-to-know-about-deathloops-story

## 10. DEATH STRANDING — body/load/world logistics + ambient human traces

Mechanism: route, terrain, carried mass, equipment and balance form one temporary system. Other players' structures and repeated paths appear asynchronously as useful traces.

Steal:
- LOADOUT evaluated against route/environment, not inventory alone.
- Body + carried object + terrain form one temporary system.
- Repeated traversal can create an emergent path.
- Other-human contribution may be ambient and useful without chat.
- Preparation objects should be justified by expected route friction.

FIELD targets: ENV-0 / BODY FIT / Human Harness exploration; Shopping→HOUSE fit plus carrying/install constraints; physical builds; HUMAN PORT bounded shared traces.

Do not steal: no likes/reputation score; digital model cannot prove safe physical load/fit.

Sources: https://www.kojimaproductions.jp/en/DSTips ; https://kojimaproductions.jp/index.php/en/death-stranding-directors-cut-beginners-guide

## 11. NIER: AUTOMATA — interface as equipped body function

Mechanism: plug-in chips turn capabilities into capacity-constrained modules; UI/world/minigame share a visual system; menu controls were deliberately constrained.

Steal:
- INTERFACE CAPABILITY can be a fitted module with explicit cost/capacity.
- Complex UI can use a tiny control grammar.
- Interface state can belong to body/agent substrate when semantically true.
- One visual language can span projections without one data model owning all of them.

FIELD targets: BODY/FIT; wearable/phone/watch cassettes; possible HUMAN HARNESS projection; mobile control reduction.

Do not steal: no literal medical/body gamification; no copying visual skin.

Source: https://www.platinumgames.com/official-blog/article/9624

## 12. RIMWORLD — anatomy → condition → capacity

Mechanism: body parts, injuries/conditions and functional capacities are distinct layers.

Steal:
- STRUCTURE != CONDITION != CAPACITY.
- Multiple structures can contribute to one capacity.
- Replacement/implant compatibility is a typed relation, not a free inventory slot.
- Capability projection may summarize function without erasing underlying structure/evidence.

FIELD targets: BODY/FIT, ENV-0, tool/robot/device health, House OS entity vs fault vs capability.

Do not steal: never import RimWorld numeric body-health semantics into personal medical interpretation.

Sources: https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Capacity ; https://rimworldwiki.com/wiki/Body_part

## 13. INTO THE BREACH — intent preview + commit boundary

Mechanism: attacks are telegraphed before commitment. Movement can be undone before firing; irreversible effect is a distinct boundary.

Steal:
- INTENT → PREVIEW → REVERSIBLE POSITIONING → COMMIT.
- Show consequence before authoritative operation when prediction is available.
- Differentiate reversible configuration from irreversible effect.
- Solve by redirecting effects, not only cancelling threats.

FIELD targets: HOUSEBUS/automation; agent actions; file/repo operations; physical controls.

Do not steal: do not imply perfect information where uncertainty exists.

## 14. FACTORIO — ghost != built

Mechanism: blueprint placement creates configuration-rich ghost entities that do not function until materials/robots build them.

Steal:
- PROPOSED GEOMETRY gets a first-class ghost state.
- Ghost may carry configuration while remaining explicitly non-real.
- Same proposal may be manually enacted or delegated to automation.
- Construction requires resources/reachability; proposal grants neither.

FIELD targets: HOUSE/ENV-0 proposed fixture vs installed object; Shopping candidate envelope; repo planned transform vs committed mutation; fabrication overlays.

Do not steal: preview/ghost never counts as physical proof.

Sources: https://wiki.factorio.com/Blueprint ; https://wiki.factorio.com/Ghost

## 15. DEAD SPACE — interface lives on/around the object

Mechanism: inventory/status are presented diegetically as functional interfaces belonging to character/world rather than detached dashboard.

Steal:
- Put status/action at the locus it controls when the locus is stable.
- Preserve world/object behind interface instead of replacing it with generic menu.
- Ornament may carry fiction; functional clarity remains primary.

FIELD targets: BODY/FIT overlays; HOUSE spatial object controls; physical-tool interfaces; user-supplied screenshot direction: annular/object-attached controls with context still visible.

Do not steal: diegesis is not permission for poor accessibility.

Source: https://inventinginteractive.com/2013/07/10/interview-dino-ignacio-dead-space/

## 16. PERSONA 5 — operation-specific graphic choreography

Mechanism: menus are shaped/animated around their action and visual identity rather than all being generic panels; response latency and animation are treated as part of usability.

Steal:
- Transition can explain hierarchy and operation.
- Asymmetry is acceptable when selection/next action remain unmistakable.
- Interaction identity can survive compression better than decorative chrome.
- Response latency is part of visual design.

FIELD targets: radial/folding fan projection; action apertures; FOLD//BLOOM transitions; mobile one-identity-owns-viewport rule.

Do not steal: no copying fonts, silhouettes, color identity or menu art.

Source: https://personacentral.com/persona-5-interview-ui-design-sound-music/

# VISUAL DONOR NOTE FROM USER-SUPPLIED SCREENSHOTS

Without asserting exact game identity, the screenshot pair demonstrates a useful UI family:
- world/object remains visible;
- interface is overlay/projection, not replacement screen;
- annular/curved geometry carries grouping and adjacency;
- large empty/dark regions preserve focus;
- thin high-contrast lines provide address/selection without card bloat;
- primary object/character remains visually sovereign;
- controls hug the active locus instead of filling every edge.

Transfer target: FIELD / BODY / HOUSE / FOLD//BLOOM should prefer object-attached apertures and annular local neighborhoods when they outperform lists. Do not universalize rings.
