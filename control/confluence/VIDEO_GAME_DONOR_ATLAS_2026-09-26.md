## Selection rule

A game earns transfer only when it offers a mechanism that solves a current FIELD problem, survives extraction from fiction/art style, preserves source/domain authority, can be stated as a testable invariant, and does not require a new sovereign app.

## 1. CITIZEN SLEEPER — bounded capacity + clocks + drives

Mechanism: each cycle exposes a limited hand of dice; the player allocates known-quality capacity to actions. Story progress is represented through clocks/drives rather than one quest tree. The design turns precarity into a visible action budget.

Steal:
- CAPACITY TOKENS rather than an infinite task list.
- CLOCK = accumulating consequence, not merely deadline.
- DRIVE = persistent direction, distinct from immediate action.
- Known uncertainty before commitment: see die value, then choose where to spend it.
- Characters/threads stay separate but coexist on one station map.

FIELD targets: Dayline bounded capacity markers; CARE/training effort/dose distinct from urgency; CURRENT direction distinct from next move; human/agent orchestration assigns scarce high-confidence execution deliberately.

Do not steal: do not gamify life with survival meters; do not turn dice quality into health/productivity score.

Sources: https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/citizen-sleeper/ ; https://www.originstory.show/episodes/citizen-sleeper ; https://www.fellowtravellerpresskit.com/citizen-sleeper

FIELD targets: Continuity/recovery; FIELD INDEX map vs unresolved-relation projection; ONE RETURN preserves learned constraints after failed branches.

## 3. RETURN OF THE OBRA DINN — identity as the hard problem

Mechanism: the deeper task is assigning stable identities to many visually present but unnamed people; the logbook combines identity and fate across scenes.

Steal:

- FACE / INSTANCE != NAME / IDENTITY.
- A boring matrix can beat an elaborate ontology when disambiguation is the task.
- Confirmation should require mutually supporting evidence, not lucky resemblance.

FIELD targets: Game of Names, CONTINUITY aliases, BODYFIELD donor-instance vs concept vs self, artifact census.

Do not steal: no alias auto-resolution from resemblance.

## 4. THE CASE OF THE GOLDEN IDOL — observation → assertion

Mechanism: exploration collects a constrained vocabulary; the player constructs explicit assertions separately.

Steal:
- OBSERVE and ASSERT are different modes/authorities.
- Vocabulary bank constrains syntax without choosing the answer.
- Partial validation can reveal proximity without revealing the wrong slot.
- Intermediate deductions avoid one giant all-or-nothing proof.

FIELD targets: CONTINUITY candidate facts → reviewed claim; FIELD INTAKE observed token bank → structured assertion; CARE observation vs interpretation; PROOF subclaims before global PASS.

Steal:
- SYSTEMS AS CONTENT: prefer a small interacting rule set over hundreds of bespoke cases.
- DECONSTRUCTION IS AN OPERATION: part removal changes support, hazard and reachability relations.
- CUT ORDER matters when effects propagate through a graph.
- Reusable modules can generate varied test fixtures without cloning logic.

FIELD targets: physical MAKE/ENV-0 assembly decomposition; repository/refactor dependency cuts; HARD EVOLUTION typed parts/ports; agent task packets assembled from tested primitives.

Do not steal: procedural variation is not proof of coverage; a generated fixture cannot stand in for an actual physical structure.

- Large combination spaces need consistent default rules plus bounded exceptions, not a special workflow per combination.
