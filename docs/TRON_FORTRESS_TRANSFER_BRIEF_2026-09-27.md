## Why this donor matters

The useful transfer is not the TRON skin. It is the way a small local rule set produces a deep strategic field:

~~~text
movement
→ leaves geometry / constraint
→ geometry changes future movement
→ speed and tolerance depend on relation to that geometry
→ team roles emerge from those coupled laws
~~~

This is especially relevant to ASCII City / Sleeper routes, ghost replay, Fold/Bloom possibility fields and multi-agent path coordination.

## Candidate mechanisms

### A. Trajectory as topology

A path may become temporary local structure rather than merely evidence of where an agent was.

Research question: does route residue alter future affordances in a way that remains legible, replayable and reversible?

Best first host: Sleeper same-world ghost replay / isolated ASCII City experiment.

Do not mutate canonical city geometry by default.

### B. Recoverable boundary margin

Fortress rubber suggests a generic relational resource:

~~~text
boundary proximity
→ spend tolerance
→ survive / maintain motion
→ tolerance recovers away from pressure
~~~

This is more interesting than a generic health bar because the resource is produced by relation to local geometry.

Keep it strictly in game/path mechanics. Do not map it to body or health semantics.

### C. Proximity coupling

Movement capacity can depend on local relational geometry rather than a fixed personal stat.

ASCII City experiment:
- travel near an established corridor gains momentum;
- leaving it restores maneuverability but loses momentum;
- this is game-space law, not transportation realism.

### D. Role emergence

A strong design lesson is that roles can emerge from shared constraints rather than explicit classes:

~~~text
same capabilities
+ different position / timing / path commitment
→ attack / defend / probe / support behavior
~~~

For future multi-agent Sleeper, prefer this over fixed role/personality taxonomies.

### E. Local law → global strategy

Before adding a new mechanic, ask:

> Can an existing local law be coupled to another existing variable so strategy appears without another mode, button or stat?

That is the main transfer to the wider FIELD.

## Sleeper / ASCII City prototype

First bounded experiment:

~~~text
same deterministic city
+ route A
+ ephemeral trail field from A
+ route B
~~~

Compare route B with and without A's residue.

Witness:

~~~text
WORLD_HASH
ROUTE_A
TRAIL_FIELD
ROUTE_B
RETURN
~~~

Success:
- route B changes for an understandable geometric reason;
- replay reconstructs the difference;
- removing the donor restores the original city exactly.

Failure:
- trail effects feel arbitrary;
- the city becomes visually obscured;
- complexity rises faster than strategic depth.
