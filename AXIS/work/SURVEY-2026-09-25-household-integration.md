## 5. Pet feeders — the actionable answer

**Nothing feeder-shaped has ever been on this LAN.** 143 parsed snapshots → 6 distinct product keys,
none feeder-shaped; a live scan the same day saw only the fan, purifier and one litter box. Their
product keys are unknown until each unit is first paired.

**But the route is already open, and it is nearly zero-effort:**

1. **Pair each feeder in Smart Life** under `amyten@gmail.com` — **no new credential gate**; that
   account is already linked to HA.
2. In HA: **Settings → Devices & Services → Tuya → ⋮ → Reload.** Devices appear.
3. Feeder-specific actions already exist in HA: **`tuya.get_feeder_meal_plan`** and
   **`tuya.set_feeder_meal_plan`**.
4. If the diagnostics JSON shows empty `status`/`status_range`/`function`, or HA warns
   *"Feeder not supported"*, functionality is gated by `tuya-device-handlers` product_id support →
   fall back to tuya-local.

**Local / cloud-free options when wanted:**
- **tuya-local** ships **43 strict dry-pet-feeder configs** plus
  `catit_pixi_6meal_feeder.yaml` (product id `wmkepdif6qzirl7a` — a **6-meal** feeder with a full DP
  map: meal plan `dp1` as a 5-byte-per-meal base64 blob, quick-feed `dp2`, feed report `dp15`,
  battery `dp11`, factory reset `dp9`). If the 6-meal unit is a Catit Pixi or a rebadge, it is
  supported out of the box.
- **Local keys without a Tuya developer account:** `vineetchoudhary/tuya-local-key` (Smart Life user
  code + QR). `tuya-cli wizard` still requires a developer account — that route is out.
- **ESPHome TuyaMCU, fully local, reversible:** proven precedent on a Tuya cat feeder — silence the
  Wi-Fi module by jumping **`EN` → GND** (no desoldering), tap the application MCU UART at **9600 8N1**
  (ESP32-S3 GPIO4 RX / GPIO5 TX), power from USB-C 5V. The MCU keeps running offline schedules.
- **Irreversible:** `tuya-cloudcutter` permanently forfeits the vendor cloud. Not without explicit consent.

## 6. RESOLVED — MQTT (was a contradiction)

`STATUS.md` and two lane reports stated **HA has no MQTT integration loaded**. **They were wrong.**
Measured directly on 2026-09-25 against HA `2026.9.3` (169 components):

- component **`mqtt` → LOADED**
- config entry **`mqtt | Mosquitto broker | loaded`**
- **`MQTT entities: NONE`**

Entity platforms, measured: `tuya 57 · hassio 14 · upnp 13 · sun 9 · backup 5 · person 2 ·
shopping_list 1 · google_translate 1 · met 1 · input_boolean 1` — **no `mqtt` platform exists.**

So the integration is fine and the gap is different, and now precisely named: the household's
topics reach HA's broker (the bridge was verified in receipt P), but nothing declares them as
entities, because MQTT entities require **either** `homeassistant/#` discovery messages **or**
manual configuration — and the household publishes a custom `home/#` namespace instead.

**The unblock is therefore: publish MQTT discovery configs for the existing topics.** No
integration to install, no new credential, and it lights up `home/cats/intent`, `home/presence`
and the phone telemetry in one move.

Related, also measured: **`telegram_bot` is absent** from HA — so the "HA hosts its own Telegram
bot" option would be a fresh install, not a flip of a switch. **`conversation` and
`assist_pipeline` ARE loaded**, so HA Assist is live and available.
