## Deletion gates

A surface can move from **DEMOTE candidate** to **VAULT** only when all are true:

1. canonical source identity round-trips;
2. address/focus round-trips;
3. selection and drafts survive;
4. aperture/scale parameters survive;
5. operator ordering survive;
6. authority ceiling is unchanged;
7. clock survives when present;
8. provenance remains inspectable;
9. unique domain behavior has a named host primitive or is listed under RESIDUE;
10. RETURN restores the exact prior interaction tuple.

## Current residue

The collapse must stop at four first-class primitives:

`CONTENT · AUTHORITY · DEPTH · TIME`

This is the anti-bloat boundary: do not invent a new app to represent these; keep them in canonical/host state and let projections declare what they suppress.
