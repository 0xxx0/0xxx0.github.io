# LAW ZOO THREAD SEAL

## What this thread became
A deliberately anti-generic family of small executable systems built around:
**tiny law → disproportionate phenotype**.

Use a new thread for the large Astra synthesis/research expedition. Keep this branch for making strange, small, live things.

## Shipped artifacts
- LAW ZOO // Field Guide — live specimens, lenses, mutation, breeding, lineage.
- PALIMPSEST — render → sample → alphabet → rerender.
- LAW DISTRICTS — traversable ASCII micro-world with different local physics.
- ONE LAW / TWO SENSES — one recurrence simultaneously drives sound + image.
- ERROR GARDEN — numerical faults are heritable traits.
- Mined law library — reusable high-leverage micro-law families.
- Field notes — mining and design notes.

## Strongest unresolved questions
- Can local laws migrate between LAW DISTRICTS rather than remain fixed by geography?
- Can PALIMPSEST converge to stable self-readable alphabets without hand-tuned targets?
- Can ONE LAW / TWO SENSES produce learnable cross-modal motifs rather than only texture?
- Can ERROR GARDEN use human dwell/selection as a non-numeric evolutionary fitness signal?
- Can LAW ZOO mutate at token/operator level while preserving executable safety?

## Five experimental hypotheses
1. **Local law > local decoration**: compare visually matched maps whose districts differ by appearance only versus actual interaction/update law; measure route recall and behavior prediction.
2. **Representation as dynamical system**: test whether PALIMPSEST produces recurrent/stable encoding attractors.
3. **Shared cause > mere synchronization**: compare audiovisual outputs driven by one recurrence versus independent tempo-synchronized processes.
4. **Error as phenotype**: compare fault-preserving versus fault-corrected ERROR GARDEN descendants for distinctiveness and voluntary replay.
5. **Tiny laws as vocabulary**: test prediction of unseen mutants after learning compact law descriptions.

## Do-not-collapse boundaries
- Do not turn LAW ZOO into a dashboard.
- Do not make all five machines share one runtime merely for architectural neatness.
- Do not force House OS / CAP-IR terminology into this branch.
- Do not treat visual similarity as mechanism equivalence.
- Do not turn artistic anomalies into neuroscience claims.
- Preserve useless-but-fertile species.
