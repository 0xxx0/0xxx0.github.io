HUMAN PORT / SOCIAL RESIDUE

REMEMBER IT
WITHOUT OWING IT.

Some conversations leave things worth returning to: an offer, a promise, a follow-up, a question, an idea. Keeping that residue does not automatically turn it into a task.
THE RULE · Conversation may create useful residue without creating an obligation.
OFFER

“I can help with that.” Keep the offer without scheduling work automatically.

FOLLOW-UP

“I said I’d look at those paintings.” Remember it, then later do, defer, release or supersede it.

IDEA

Something useful appeared in the conversation. Send the durable idea to Idea Atlas.

QUESTION

An unresolved question may be more valuable than a task. Keep it open without forcing action.
