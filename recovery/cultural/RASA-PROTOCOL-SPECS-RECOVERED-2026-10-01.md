## A001 — Series Grammar v1

:::writing{variant="document" id="58321"}
# SERIES GRAMMAR v1
**Child of Storms / Confluence Visual Operating Language**

**Purpose**  
Generate visually coherent, experimentally variable plates that convert abstract systems into inspectable operations. Every plate must function simultaneously as image, map, interface, and compressed argument.

## 1. VISUAL INVARIANTS

Every canonical plate contains:

1. **One dominant transformation**
   - filter, route, merge, escape, verify, compress, stabilize, integrate.
   - Never organize a plate around a topic noun alone.

2. **One central attractor**
   - vortex, field, body-map, lattice, basin, reticle, topology, wheel.
   - Occupies approximately the perceptual center and carries the primary causal structure.

3. **Peripheral operational modules**
   - 4–9 modules surrounding the attractor.
   - Each answers one distinct question: input, state, transformation, metric, failure, test, action, legend.

4. **Visible flow**
   - Arrows, paths, gradients, node transitions, before/after states.
   - Static taxonomy is subordinate to transformation.

5. **A compact glyph language**
   - Recurrent primitives for source, node, gate, threshold, flow, branch, merge, loop, test, release.

6. **A clear title architecture**
   - DOMAIN / OPERATION
   - functional subtitle
   - sequence number.

---

## 2. COMPOSITIONAL GRAMMAR

**Hierarchy**

1. Title
2. Central mechanism
3. Primary operational loop
4. High-value side modules
5. Supporting metrics / legends
6. Micro-detail

The eye should understand the plate at three distances:

**3 m** → title + dominant structure  
**1 m** → major causal architecture  
**30 cm** → procedures, evidence, annotations

No detail may exist solely to simulate complexity.

---

## 3. PALETTE

**Ground**  
Near-black / charcoal.

**Primary semantic light**  
Warm off-white for canonical structure and readable text.

**Cool channel**  
Electric ultramarine / deep cobalt:
signal, sensing, opening, optionality, input, exploratory flow.

**Warm channel**  
Ember orange:
constraint, energy, friction, transformation, intervention, risk.

**Gold**  
Restrained:
geometry, borders, stable relationships, ritual / canonical layer.

**Rule**  
Color carries meaning before decoration.

Never introduce a new accent color without assigning it a stable semantic role.

---

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY

**T0 — Plate title**  
Large condensed uppercase. Maximum 2–4 words.

**T1 — Functional subtitle**  
Small spaced uppercase. Identifies transformation and plate number.

**T2 — Module title**  
Short uppercase noun or verb phrase.

**T3 — Operational label**  
Prefer verbs: ROUTE, TEST, RELEASE, VERIFY.

**T4 — Annotation**  
One concise sentence or phrase.

**T5 — Data / provenance**  
Smallest type but never optional when quantitative or evidentiary content appears.

Avoid prose blocks inside generated imagery.

Ideal semantic payload:
- title: ≤4 words
- module heading: ≤3 words
- labels: 1–3 words
- annotations: usually ≤10 words

Long explanations belong outside the generated image or in post-typeset editions.

---

## 5. MODULE TYPES

Use only modules with a defined job.

**M1 INPUT**  
Sources, stimuli, constraints, incoming variables.

**M2 FIELD / STATE**  
Current topology, condition, distribution.

**M3 TRANSFORMATION**  
What changes what.

**M4 ROUTE**  
Possible paths, branches, gates, alternatives.

**M5 FAILURE**  
Drift, noise, trap, bottleneck, false exit.

**M6 MEASURE**  
Observable state or genuinely calculated quantity.

**M7 TEST**  
Falsifier, probe, validation condition.

**M8 ACTION**  
Next operation or intervention.

**M9 MEMORY**  
History, recurrence, persistence, previous state.

**M10 LEGEND / PROVENANCE**  
Symbol meaning, epistemic status, source lineage.

A plate does not need all ten.

---

## 6. DENSITY RULES

**Macro density**  
One dominant visual proposition only.

**Meso density**  
4–9 distinct secondary modules.

**Micro density**  
High detail is allowed only where it resolves structure.

**Density test**  
Every added mark must do at least one:
- distinguish
- connect
- measure
- orient
- warn
- instruct

Otherwise delete it.

**Legibility rule**  
Complexity may increase recursively; ambiguity may not.

**Data rule**  
Never invent numeric precision to make a plate appear technical.

If no real number exists:
use ordinal states, qualitative bands, blank fields, or clearly marked schematic values.

---

## 7. FRACTAL / RECURSIVE LOGIC

“Fractal fluency” means recurrence across scale, not decorative fractal texture.

Preferred operations:
- nested representations
- local ↔ global correspondence
- recursive pathing
- repeated motifs with controlled variation
- scale-dependent simplification
- branching and reconvergence
- partial space-filling curves

“1.4D” remains an aesthetic / design hypothesis unless a particular plate explicitly defines and derives a mathematical interpretation.

Never present decorative equations as established mathematics.

---

## 8. VARIATION AXES

Preserve invariants while varying:

**Geometry**  
radial ↔ axial ↔ branching ↔ lattice ↔ topographic ↔ anatomical

**Field behavior**  
convergent ↔ divergent ↔ turbulent ↔ periodic ↔ recursive

**Information mode**  
map ↔ dashboard ↔ protocol ↔ atlas ↔ diagnostic ↔ scorecard

**Dominant rasa / affective register**  
wonder ↔ tension ↔ courage ↔ tranquility ↔ compassion

**Spatial emphasis**  
center-heavy ↔ vertical journey ↔ distributed network ↔ nested scales

**Representation**
literal schematic ↔ abstract topology ↔ controlled metaphor.

Do not vary palette, typography, geometry, information architecture, and semantic vocabulary simultaneously.

---

## 9. SEQUENCING LOGIC

Canonical sequence is transformational:

01 FIELD  
→ encounter complexity

02 ATTENTION  
→ select signal

03 SCALE  
→ navigate representation

04 CONFLUENCE  
→ combine flows

05 ACTION  
→ convert understanding into intervention

06 CONSTRAINT / EXIT  
→ detect and alter limiting topology

07 EMBODIMENT / WITNESS  
→ return representation to lived perception

08 INTEGRATION  
→ combine the operating primitives

09 VERIFICATION  
→ determine what can actually be trusted

Future plates should extend capability, not merely add themes.

A new plate earns a number only if it introduces a new transformation unavailable in earlier plates.

---

## 10. DEFINITION OF DONE

A plate is canonical only when:

- dominant transformation is immediately identifiable;
- central diagram performs real explanatory work;
- every module has a distinct operational role;
- hierarchy survives thumbnail view;
- microtext is legible and proofed;
- quantitative content has provenance;
- metaphors are not disguised as mechanisms;
- epistemic status is visible where necessary;
- viewer can derive at least one observation, test, or action from the plate;
- removing any major module would reduce understanding.

**Kernel**

Beauty attracts attention.  
Structure routes it.  
Evidence determines what survives.

## A002 — Semantic Validation Schema v1

:::writing{variant="document" id="74106"}
# SEMANTIC VALIDATION SCHEMA v1
**Epistemic Type System for Visual Plates**

**Purpose**  
Prevent visual polish, formal notation, or quantitative appearance from silently upgrading a statement beyond its evidence.

Every substantive element receives exactly one primary epistemic class.

---

# 1. PRIMARY CLASSES

## P — POETIC

**Definition**  
Metaphor, symbolic compression, aesthetic proposition, invocation.

**Example**  
“The storm teaches pattern.”

**Allowed use**  
Titles, framing language, conceptual bridges, image metaphor.

**Not allowed to imply**  
causal mechanism, empirical fact, quantitative prediction.

**Visual marker**  
`P`

---

## H — HEURISTIC

**Definition**  
Useful rule, model, protocol, or approximation whose value is practical rather than universally proven.

**Example**  
“Reduce input before increasing filtering complexity.”

**Required support**  
State intended context and known limits when consequential.

**Visual marker**  
`H`

---

## D — DERIVED

**Definition**  
Result obtained logically or mathematically from explicit premises or source data.

**Required record**
- inputs
- transformation / formula
- assumptions
- output

**Example**  
Priority score calculated from declared weighted variables.

**Visual marker**  
`D`

A derivation can be valid while its premises remain uncertain.

---

## T — TESTED

**Definition**  
Claim or mechanism examined against an explicit test, observation, experiment, or repeated application.

**Required record**
- test performed
- test conditions
- observed result
- failures / exceptions
- date or version where relevant

**Visual marker**  
`T`

“Tested” does not mean universally true.

---

## V — VERIFIED

**Definition**  
Claim strongly supported by appropriate, traceable evidence and independently checkable within its stated scope.

**Required record**
- source / provenance
- scope
- verification method
- relevant uncertainty
- freshness where time-sensitive

**Visual marker**  
`V`

Verified status is never inferred from authority, typography, citation count, or repetition.

---

# 2. AUXILIARY STATES

Use only when needed.

`△` UNCERTAIN  
Evidence exists but confidence or interpretation remains materially unresolved.

`∅` UNKNOWN  
Required information is unavailable.

`S` SCHEMATIC  
Value or diagram is illustrative rather than measured.

`X` CONTESTED  
Credible incompatible interpretations remain live.

`E` ESTIMATE  
Approximate value derived from incomplete or noisy inputs.

These modify a primary class; they do not replace it.

Example:

`H + △` → plausible heuristic with substantial uncertainty.

`D + E` → calculated estimate.

`P + S` → purely schematic conceptual representation.

---

# 3. VALIDATION OBJECT

Every substantive element should be representable as:

**CLAIM**  
What is asserted?

**TYPE**  
P / H / D / T / V

**SOURCE**  
Where did it come from?

**TRANSFORMATION**  
Was it interpreted, calculated, summarized, or extrapolated?

**SCOPE**  
Where does it apply?

**UNCERTAINTY**  
What could make it wrong?

**FALSIFIER / CHECK**  
What observation would change its status?

---

# 4. QUANTITATIVE RULE

Every displayed number must be one of:

1. **Observed**
2. **Derived**
3. **Estimated**
4. **Schematic**

No fifth category exists.

If schematic, visually mark `S` or use obviously non-numeric qualitative representation.

Never generate:
- arbitrary percentages
- decorative confidence scores
- fictional precision
- unexplained index values
- pseudo-measurements.

---

# 5. FORMULA RULE

An equation must be:

**Established**  
known formal relationship with source.

**Derived**  
constructed transparently from explicit assumptions.

**Experimental**  
proposed model clearly labeled as such.

**Schematic**  
visual analogy, not mathematics.

Typography must not blur these classes.

---

# 6. METAPHOR FIREWALL

A metaphor may generate:

- a question;
- a representation;
- a hypothesis;
- a heuristic.

A metaphor cannot by itself establish:

- a mechanism;
- a fact;
- a causal relationship;
- a quantitative model.

Required transition:

POETIC  
→ hypothesis / heuristic  
→ operational definition  
→ test  
→ evidence  
→ possible verification.

Never skip stages silently.

---

# 7. STATUS TRANSITIONS

`P → H`  
when symbolic language becomes an explicitly bounded practical rule.

`H → T`  
when the heuristic is operationalized and tested.

`D → T`  
when a derived prediction encounters observation.

`T → V`  
when evidence becomes sufficiently strong, traceable, reproducible, and scoped.

`V → △`  
when contradictory or newer evidence appears.

Statuses are reversible.

---

# 8. VISUAL ENCODING

For dense plates use a small provenance strip or corner tag.

Example:

`P` POETIC  
`H` HEURISTIC  
`D` DERIVED  
`T` TESTED  
`V` VERIFIED

For important nodes, place the type beside the claim itself.

Numbers receive explicit provenance:

`72 [D]`  
`HIGH [H]`  
`0.41 [E+D]`  
`SCHEMATIC [P+S]`

Do not encode epistemic strength solely through color; labels must remain interpretable in monochrome.

---

# 9. PLATE-LEVEL AUDIT

Before publication ask:

1. Which elements merely look scientific?
2. Which numbers lack derivation?
3. Which causal arrows are actually metaphors?
4. Which statements have no scope?
5. Which heuristics are phrased as laws?
6. Which citations support only adjacent claims?
7. Which status would change if contrary evidence appeared?
8. Does any visual hierarchy imply stronger confidence than the evidence warrants?

Any unresolved answer must remain visible as uncertainty.

---

# 10. DEFINITION OF VALID

A plate passes semantic validation when:

- every substantive claim has a class;
- every number has provenance;
- every formula has status;
- inference is distinguishable from source material;
- uncertainty remains visible;
- contradiction is preserved rather than averaged away;
- aesthetic salience does not exceed evidentiary warrant without explicit symbolic framing;
- a reader can reconstruct why each high-impact claim is present.

**Kernel**

Representation may compress evidence.  
It may not silently upgrade it.
:::

## A003 — Plate 09 Specification

:::writing{variant="document" id="36492"}
# PLATE 09 — PROVENANCE ENGINE
## EPISTEMIC CONTROL / VERIFICATION FIELD // 09

**Role in sequence**

01–08 build the operator.

09 installs the instrument panel that tells the operator which parts of the map are observation, model, metaphor, test, or fact.

This is not another subject plate.

It is the plate that governs all previous and future plates.

---

# 1. DOMINANT TRANSFORMATION

**UNCLASSIFIED ASSERTION → TRACEABLE CLAIM**

The plate should visually transform a chaotic field of attractive statements, numbers, arrows, and diagrams into a smaller set of claims with explicit lineage and status.

Core operation:

**TRACE → CLASSIFY → TEST → CONTRADICT → CALIBRATE → DECIDE**

---

# 2. CENTRAL ATTRACTOR

## CLAIM-LINEAGE GRAPH

Central composition: a large vertically oriented branching network.

Left / outer field:
raw inputs

→ observations  
→ sources  
→ testimony  
→ measurements  
→ metaphors  
→ assumptions

Middle:
claim nodes

Right / inner convergence:
status-controlled decision surface.

Every important claim has visible incoming ancestry.

No floating conclusions.

---

# 3. PRIMARY LOOP

### 1 TRACE
Where did this claim enter?

### 2 TYPE
P / H / D / T / V

### 3 SCOPE
Where does it apply?

### 4 TEST
What would change our mind?

### 5 CONTRADICT
What evidence pushes the other way?

### 6 CALIBRATE
How strong is the surviving claim?

### 7 ACT
What decision does the current evidence justify?

Outer return loop:

**NEW EVIDENCE → REOPEN**

Verification is not terminal.

---

# 4. MODULE ARCHITECTURE

## M1 — CLAIM INBOX
Raw statements entering the system.

Labels:
OBSERVATION
SOURCE
MODEL
METAPHOR
ASSUMPTION
MEASUREMENT

---

## M2 — EPISTEMIC TYPE STACK

Five vertically ordered classes:

P POETIC  
H HEURISTIC  
D DERIVED  
T TESTED  
V VERIFIED

Do not visually imply that P is “bad” and V is “good.”

They answer different questions.

---

## M3 — PROVENANCE TRACE

Small node graph:

SOURCE  
→ EXTRACTION  
→ TRANSFORMATION  
→ CLAIM  
→ DECISION

Expose missing links.

Status:
COMPLETE
PARTIAL
BROKEN
UNKNOWN

---

## M4 — CONTRADICTION FIELD

Two or more incompatible claim trajectories remain simultaneously visible.

Labels:

SUPPORTS  
CHALLENGES  
INDEPENDENT  
DEPENDENT  
STALE  
UNRESOLVED

No forced synthesis.

---

## M5 — CONFIDENCE ≠ AUTHORITY

A small comparison panel explicitly separates:

CONFIDENCE  
SOURCE AUTHORITY  
EVIDENCE QUALITY  
INDEPENDENCE  
FRESHNESS

Show these as orthogonal dimensions rather than one composite score.

No invented numbers.

Use qualitative bands unless actual measurements exist.

---

## M6 — FALSIFIER / TEST

Each major claim maps to:

WHAT WOULD WE EXPECT?

WHAT WOULD DISCONFIRM IT?

WHAT CAN WE TEST NOW?

WHAT REMAINS UNOBSERVABLE?

This is the principal action module.

---

## M7 — STATUS TRANSITIONS

Diagram:

P → H → T  
D → T → V

with reverse arrows:

V → △  
T → H  
H → ∅

Labels:

UPGRADE REQUIRES EVIDENCE  
DOWNGRADE ON CONTRADICTION

---

## M8 — SATURATION DETECTOR

Purpose:
prevent repeated agreement from masquerading as increasing confidence.

Inputs:

NEW EVIDENCE?  
NEW HYPOTHESIS?  
NEW FALSIFIER?  
DECISION CHANGED?

If all = NO:

**STOP ITERATING**

This module should be unusually prominent.

---

## M9 — DECISION GATE

Final output classes:

ACT  
TEST FIRST  
HOLD  
REJECT  
REOPEN

Decision depends on evidence + consequence, not certainty alone.

---

# 5. CENTRAL VISUAL METAPHOR

A luminous network enters from many directions.

At first:
dense, tangled, visually seductive.

As it crosses successive gates:
lineage becomes visible,
unsupported connections disappear,

The visual climax is subtraction.

# 11. USER OPERATION

The plate should allow a viewer to take any sentence from plates 01–08 and ask:

1. What type of statement is this?
2. Where did it come from?
3. What transformation produced it?
4. What contradicts it?
5. What would test it?
6. What decision does it justify now?

If these cannot be answered, the statement returns to the unclassified field.

- poetic material is visually deprecated;
- contradiction is collapsed prematurely;
- authority and evidence are merged;
- confidence is represented as a single universal number;
- generated decoration imitates provenance without actual lineage;
- the plate cannot audit itself.
