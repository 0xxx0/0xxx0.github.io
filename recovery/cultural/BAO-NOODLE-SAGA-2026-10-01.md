## The mechanism (what the saga actually is)

**Not one joke. A serial war of mistaken identity.** Every beat has the same shape:

> a character is beaten for a **visual feature they cannot help**, because the attacker
> believes it is a disguise worn by someone else.

| part | victim | beaten for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 泡麵 instant noodle | *"you permed your hair"* |
| 2 | 小籠包 xiaolongbao | *"acting cute... wearing children's clothes"* |
| 3 | 割包 gua bao | *"a MIDDLE PART — who does he think he is, a Japanese idol?"* |
| 4 | 米粉 rice noodle | *"if you burnt your own hair, stay home"* |
| 5 | **金針菇 enoki** | *"wearing a hat"* |
| 6 | **肉丸子 meatball** | *"STREAKING"* (no wrapper) |
| 7 | 叉燒包 char siu bao | *"coughing blood"* |
| 8 | 薯條 fries | *"dressed like a disco ball"* |
| 9 | 義大利麵 spaghetti | *"a blood-stained shirt"* |
| 10 | **麵包 bread** | **"his position is unclear" — 面 AND 包, both sides beat him** |
| 11 | 饅頭 mantou | *"you went and got a face lift"* |

**The engine is the pun, but the subject is not the pun.** 包 = bun *and* wrap. 面 = noodle *and*
face. 燙 = scald *and* perm. The saga is about **being attacked for what you look like**, and its
purest figure is **麵包 — bread — beaten by both clans because it is both things at once.**

**And nobody in fifteen parts ever stops judging by appearance.** That is the whole of it.

## The Singapore line, verbatim (sgforums, 2003)

> *"Char Siew Pao and Mee Kia got involved in an argument. Char Siew Pao got very angry and shouted
> at Mee Kia, 'I'm going to find my gang to hantam you!'*
> *So Char Siew Pao went to round up **Leng Yong Pau and Tau Sar Pow**.*
> *Just then, **Maggi Mee** walked around the corner. Immediately, the Paos started to beat him up.*
> *As Char Siew Pao was punching Maggi Mee, he shouted, **'Don't think just because you perm your
> hair, we can't recognize you, okay!'**"*

## The sequel — "Revenge of the mees" (Time Out)

Maggi Goreng, beaten, gathers the Noodle Clan. They capture Small Bao and tie him to a chair.
**Char Kway Teow, the noodle boss, bursts in:** *"Don't touch him! That one is our undercover agent,
**Yin Si Juan**!"* — **銀絲卷, silver-thread roll.** The bun was a noodle the whole time.

## The honest gap

- **15 parts recovered of an estimated 40.** The pixnet collector had 15; the "40+" figure came from
  a research lane's estimate, **not from a source I have seen.** The rest are on dead blogs or were
  never written down.
- **No Chinese-language source beyond part 15 was located.**
- **The SG and CN traditions are related but not identical** — the SG version localises to hawker
  names (Maggi Goreng, mee kia, tau sar bao); the CN version has the mushroom/meatball/spaghetti
  branches. **Do not merge them into one lineage.** (I did exactly that earlier and it was wrong.)
