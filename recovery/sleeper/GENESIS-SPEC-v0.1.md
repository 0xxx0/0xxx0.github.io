## 00 — Constitution

The Atlas is governed by twelve laws.

1. **Source before synthesis.**
2. **Absence must remain visible.**
3. **Quotation and reconstruction must never share an unlabeled surface.**
4. **Canon, branch, tonal study, and rejected draft are distinct states.**
5. **No branch may silently absorb another.**
6. **Every symbol requires an operation or remains decorative.**
7. **Every protocol requires an observable result.**
8. **Every artifact preserves a route to its sources.**
9. **Comedy may puncture false solemnity but not consequence.**
10. **Revision changes status; it does not rewrite history.**
11. **Portable continuation requires an explicit allowed-inference zone.**
12. **The canon exists to increase usable freedom, not merely internal consistency.**

# VI. Demonstration unit

## SU-FM-005 — W8 Does Not Move

### Story

The Sleeper found W8 blocking the only door.

“Move,” said the Sleeper.

W8 did not move.

The Sleeper pushed. Negotiated. Threatened to redesign the room.

W8 remained where consequence had placed it.

The Pajama Pugilist arrived carrying tea and one boxing glove.

“You keep treating the constraint as an insult,” they said.

“It is in my way.”

“Yes. That is what constraints look like from the side that wants something.”

The Sleeper stopped pushing.

They measured the door.  
Measured W8.  
Measured what they carried.

Then they left three unnecessary things on the floor and passed through the space that had always remained.

W8 had not moved.

The Sleeper had.

### Operator

**Anchor:** establish the real constraint before redesigning reality around resentment.

### Protocol — WEIGHT

```text
W — What is immovable within the present decision window?
E — Evidence: how do we know?
I — Impact: what does it actually prevent?
G — Give up: what can be removed, deferred, or reduced?
H — Hinge: what smallest change creates passage?
T — Test the passage.
```

### Measure

- time from blockage to first valid experiment;
- assumptions invalidated;
- scope removed;
- whether the constraint truly remained fixed;
- whether the chosen passage preserved the purpose.

### Transfer

- **Project:** reduce scope before extending deadline.
- **House:** distinguish an actual limit from unwillingness.
- **Code:** profile bottleneck before architectural rewrite.
- **Body:** adapt training load rather than denying pain signals.
- **Archive:** preserve source limits instead of inventing continuity.

S=A Field Manual unit succeeds when its story becomes a remembered move under pressure.

# VII. Nine-Gate Test v2

The previous test needed sharper pass conditions.

Score each gate:

- **0** absent;
- **1** asserted;
- **2** demonstrated.

| Gate | Required question | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Provenance** | Where did this come from? | source or explicit proposed status |
| **Truth** | What is observed, inferred, invented? | separated claims |
| **Compression** | What complexity does it reduce? | before/after representation |
| **Retrieval** | Can it be recalled when needed? | cue, index, mnemonic |
| **Operation** | What can a person do with it? | executable verb |
| **Measure** | What result changes confidence? | observable indicator |
| **Transfer** | Where else does the mechanism work? | one successful translation |
| **Resilience** | What happens under missing data, error, or stress? | fallback or degradation rule |
| **Return** | Can we trace, revise, reject, and restore? | revision history |

## Admission thresholds

```text
0–7   aesthetic fragment
8–11  exploratory prototype
12–14 usable instrument
15–16 robust instrument
17–18 canon candidate
```

### Hard failure rules

Regardless of total score, an artifact cannot become canon with:

- Provenance = 0;
- Truth = 0;
- Operation = 0;
- Return = 0.

Beauty cannot compensate for falsified memory.
