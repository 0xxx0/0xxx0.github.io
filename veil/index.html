What this measures. PSE is the interval your clock calls “equal” to 600 ms — the bisection point. Tap SD is your timing precision at 60 bpm. Both are state-dependent: arousal, temperature, caffeine and breath shift them.

Limits. A phone screen refreshes at 60–120 Hz and setTimeout jitters by a few ms; that is why the instrument records the interval it actually presented, and why it reports within-person deltas rather than absolute thresholds. Headphones help the tap test; silence helps the bisection.

Protocol. Same time of day, same light, no caffeine inside 90 minutes. Baseline → 5 minutes at ~5.5 breaths/min → retest. The number to watch is the shift in PSE, not the PSE itself.
