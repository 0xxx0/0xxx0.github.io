# RESPONSE PRINCIPLES / RESEARCH DONORS

Status: **design donor**, not medical or psychological advice.

The Laconic/Iconic instrument should not infer a person's diagnosis, motives, or hidden emotional state from sparse text. It may preserve what was actually said, expose tentative possibilities as tentative, and help choose a response move.

## R1 / RESPONSIVENESS

**Design translation:** a useful supportive reply should make it easier for the other person to feel understood, validated, and cared for. Do not equate validation with agreement, and do not manufacture certainty about what they feel.

Useful source:
- Arican-Dinc B, Gable SL. *Responsiveness in romantic partners' interactions.* Current Opinion in Psychology. 2023;53:101652. DOI: 10.1016/j.copsyc.2023.101652.
- Reis lab PPRS materials: https://sites.rochester.edu/reis/research-measures/

Instrument rule:
`OBSERVED → POSSIBLE → MOVE`

Never:
`OBSERVED → DIAGNOSIS`

## R2 / SUPPORT MODE IS A CHOICE

People seek and receive several kinds of interpersonal emotion regulation: problem solving/reappraisal, encouraging sharing, affection, distraction/other strategies, and sometimes invalidating responses.

**Design translation:** before solving, make the response mode selectable where context is ambiguous:

`WITNESS / THINK / HELP / PLAY / SILENCE`

Source:
- Liu DY, Strube MJ, Thompson RJ. *Interpersonal Emotion Regulation: an Experience Sampling Study.* Affective Science. 2021;2(3):273–288. DOI: 10.1007/s42761-021-00044-y. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9382965/

## R3 / VALIDATION IS NOT A UNIVERSAL SCRIPT

Experimental work suggests validation/invalidation effects can differ by emotion and person. This is a reason to avoid canned "your feelings are valid" output as an unconditional reflex.

**Design translation:** reflect the concrete experience first; label emotions tentatively; offer choice.

Source:
- Kuo JR et al. *The who and what of validation: an experimental examination of validation and invalidation of specific emotions and the moderating effect of emotion dysregulation.* Borderline Personality Disorder and Emotion Dysregulation. 2022;9:15. DOI: 10.1186/s40479-022-00185-x. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9116024/

## R4 / GRANULARITY OVER GENERIC SENTIMENT

Emotional granularity means making precise, context-sensitive distinctions rather than collapsing experience into broad "good/bad" labels.

**Design translation:** when naming emotion is useful, prefer a small tentative distinction:
`frustrated / ashamed / lonely / exhausted / afraid / uncertain`
rather than pretending certainty or using one generic empathy template.

Source:
- Wilson-Mendenhall CD, Dunne JD. *Cultivating Emotional Granularity.* Frontiers in Psychology. 2021;12:703658. DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2021.703658. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8751584/

## R5 / GOOD NEWS NEEDS A DIFFERENT ENGINE

Research on capitalization distinguishes active-constructive responses from merely positive but passive responses. The useful mechanism is **enthusiasm + elaboration**.

**Design translation:** when someone shares good news:
`CELEBRATE → ASK / ELABORATE → SAVOR`
rather than immediately minimizing, warning, fixing, or changing topic.

Source:
- Kashdan TB et al. *Failure to capitalize on sharing good news with romantic partners.* Journal of Anxiety Disorders. 2013. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3776926/

## R6 / HUMOR IS A BRANCH, NOT A DEFAULT PERSONA

Archive evidence shows the original Laconic/Iconic work used humor effectively when the other person opted into a playful branch, and support trees offered serious / silly / practical / silence routes.

**Design translation:** establish contact first when stakes are unclear; then let humor increase maneuverability. Humor must not erase the live issue.

## PUBLIC / PRIVATE BOUNDARY

Private archived examples may inform a mechanism without their sensitive facts entering the public repository. A public scene derived from such a source is marked **RECONSTRUCTED** at scene level, and any newly written public lines remain **NEW**.

No diagnostic classifier. No sentiment score as truth. No auto-send.
