THE HINGE.

the smallest difference that changes the analysis

Two versions. One small change. Find the one that changes what anyone can do with it.

01 · the drill

ruleDo not maximise detail. Preserve the detail that changes possible action. Three fictional cases. Each has two versions of one clause and three candidate differences. Only one difference changes what anyone can conclude, compare or do — pick it.

02 · the signal test

signal testAsk one question of any taxonomy, note or analysis: would merging two fields allow opposite answers to look identical? If yes, it is below the needed resolution — and the difference you dropped is a hinge.
the dialA broad label plateaus because different questions share one container. The fix is mechanical: unfold the broad point into the separate questions inside it, then pin each answer to its source language.

03 · the lab

the lab checks nothing for you — it makes you check.

Free mode. Paste two versions of anything — clauses, specs, tasting notes, replies. The lab computes a local word-level difference and nothing else. It marks what changed; you decide which change is the hinge, tick the frames it reaches, and write the receipt.
