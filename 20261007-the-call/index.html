how verdicts workNone of them judge you. A verdict names what a call costs and what it buys — or, where the rule is the point, what it does to the world. There is no score that matters, no fail state, and nothing here is called wrong.
who this is forIt is for you. There is no export, no team dashboard, and no way for anyone with authority over you to read your result. That is not a privacy footnote — it is the reason an instrument is an instrument and not a performance review wearing a friendly hat.
