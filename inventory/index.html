THE INVENTORY

7 systems hiding behind 7 recurring names · every entry grounded in the registry, not guessed · generated from showcase-manifest.json
Why this page exists. Forty-seven files across this repo share seven names — lens, trace, map, glyph, aperture, audit, health — and nothing said which were load-bearing and which were parked.

They are not clutter. They are seven distinct systems, each doing one job, several of them small and most of them finished. The confusion was that they were never named as families. Now they are. Each description below is quoted from the surface's own registered entry.
